Kagoshima Sushi, Edomae Style

A calm, classic counter experience at our main branch in Kajiyacho (加治屋町), Kagoshima City. Takeout-only shop on Bunka-dori for sushi on your way home.

Call to reserve (main) Takeout location

Two ways to enjoy Edokko Sushi

Main branch (dine-in) — Near Kajiyacho-no-Mori Park (加治屋まちの杜公園). Nigiri, sashimi, and a variety of cooked dishes in a relaxed setting. Reservations and delivery conditions are handled by phone. Hours (as provided): 17:00–21:00 L.O., closed Sundays.

Bunka-dori (takeout) — Edomae-quality sushi for home or your hotel. Popular items reportedly include shrimp–lettuce rolls, tekka-maki, and chawanmushi. Hours: from 17:30 until sold out (late night as locally defined), closed Sundays.
